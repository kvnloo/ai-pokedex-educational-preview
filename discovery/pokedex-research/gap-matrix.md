# Evidence gaps and boundaries

This is a function-focused pass, not an exhaustive inventory of every game, device or episode. Verification labels describe the evidence actually available; no episode footage was inspected. The source ledger records the canonical synthesis and parent-confirmed access details, not a second independent source audit. Sword/Shield, Arceus, Sun, Scarlet/Violet and both Pokédex 3D pages had full-page reads. Diamond uses extracted official manual text without PDF screenshots. Alpha Sapphire is supporting, unverified detail. Alolan Open House is indexed official text only.
