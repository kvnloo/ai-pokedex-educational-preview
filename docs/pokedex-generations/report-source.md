# Ash's device collection: reference baseline

Prepared 2026-09-05 for the existing Pokémon game. This is the modeling and interaction baseline, not a claim that the eight assets are finished. The supplied board concepts remain separate from the anime-era collection.

## Physical identity

Root inspected the downloaded anime frames. Their bytes and source URLs are recorded in `canonical/devices/generations/era-reference-manifest.json`; the images remain private under `/mnt/zer0models/pokemon-iac/devices/pokedex/eras/references`. These are screenshots of the anime hosted by a community archive, not official orthographic manufacturing drawings. Captions are secondary evidence. Hands, cropping and stylized perspective leave gaps in the unseen geometry.

## Functions and progression

The anime's established pattern is spoken identification plus manual lookup. Its entries are generally preloaded, so species knowledge need not wait for a capture. Trainer identification and registration also belong to the device's role. Unknown-species responses should remain possible. Storage transfer is described in dialogue but not demonstrated by the cited overview; it is not a verified physical feature. These are secondary-source findings to use as a functional baseline, with exact episode checks still needed for fine detail. [Anime Pokédex overview](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9dex#In_animation)

Alola adds an expressive, learning companion. The episode history documents video import/recording, a later voice-sampling upgrade, and database updates. These should unlock at appropriate story beats instead of appearing as universal capabilities of every older device. Rotom enters Ash's empty Dex in *Loading the Dex!*. [Rotom Dex episode history](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Rotom_Pok%C3%A9dex_(anime))

Journeys uses a new phone from Professor Cerise, possessed by one of his Rotom. It supports local information, a numbered species list and World Coronation Series coordination. Do not silently identify this Rotom with the Alola companion or import the later Horizons tablet transformation into Ash's phone. [Rotom Phone animation history](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Rotom_Phone#Pokémon_Journeys:_The_Series)

The official pages for [Loading the Dex!](https://www.pokemon.com/us/animation/seasons/20/episode-3-loading-the-dex) and [Ivysaur's Mysterious Tower!](https://www.pokemon.com/us/animation/seasons/23/episode-3-ivysaurs-mysterious-tower) resolved but exposed only an iframe to the research reader. Their episode content has not been independently verified here. The research ledger marks those gaps explicitly.

Kevin's game rule is separate from those canon claims. Entering an era grants its device family once. Prior devices and chosen visual candidates remain in the collection. Reviewing a candidate does not mark it canon, story-unlocked, or visually approved. Keep the Kalos upgrade under its own variant identity; track any later Kanto revision separately when its reference set is obtained.

## Next modeling decision

Kanto and Johto are the first canonical pair. Kanto has enough visible structure for an initial dimensional study; Johto needs an uncropped open/closed sequence before committing to its cover mechanism. Millimeter dimensions, internal bearings, wall thickness and tolerances will be explicit engineering interpretations. Each pair must retain native-camera comparisons, solid geometry checks and actual runtime playback evidence before its review batch is marked complete.
