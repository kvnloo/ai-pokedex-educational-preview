# Root-surface diagnosis

The useful next action is local cleanup of r02d's intersecting collar strip. The PCA-based surface fit removes most of the visible ribs without material overall shrinkage. The remaining numerical defect is tightly localized. No geometry or vertex-position proposal was produced by this audit.

The original profile and source-side surface derivatives carry circumferential irregularity longitudinally through the Hermite collar. In r02a, radial Fourier modes 8–127 have RMS .001485 at the attachment, .001557 at generated ring 1, .000173 at ring 5, and .00000722 at the unchanged circular ring 6. This progression supports a shared profile/derivative source for the ribs. It is not merely shading from the original cut. These measurements describe geometric variation after broad ovality modes are excluded; they do not classify anatomy.

The native r02b pass was too weak and uneven to remove that pattern. It moved 3747 of 9902 selected vertices. Even among 5510 vertices with weight above .99, only 2148 moved and median displacement was zero. Of 317 fully weighted attachment vertices, only four moved. Generated ring 1's high-mode RMS fell by approximately six percent, while the attachment remained unchanged. The original root's Y=.05 high-mode RMS changed by only .12 percent. The measurements establish the ineffective result; this audit does not claim to have traced Blender's internal solver reason for exact stationary vertices.

In r02c, independently fitted displacements along raw mesh vertex normals produced 48 reversed triangles. Their median original minimum altitude was .000143, versus .003775 across the affected region. Their median original vertex-normal spread was 14.38 degrees, versus 2.91 degrees overall. Several source and attachment triangles had minimum altitudes between .000006 and .000022. Such triangles can invert under tiny differences between neighboring displacements, even when every individual movement is described as normal-only. The larger local intersection test reports two pairs in r02a and fifty in r02c.

r02d's PCA frames remove those orientation reversals. Its signed volume changes by +.005466 percent, surface area by -.028937 percent, and sampled Y-section areas by -.199467 to +.122834 percent. Normal-only fitting does not guarantee volume preservation, but these measurements provide no evidence of material overall shrinkage in this candidate. All 112475 protected vertex coordinates, their oriented triangle triples, and the flame's world geometry stay exact.

r02d still introduces twelve confirmed intersections, all within one narrow collar strip. They involve eighteen stable vertices and sixteen triangles. Their triangle bounds are X[-.504774,-.484936], Y[.149250,.250910], Z[-.475182,-.466104]. The original pair clearances were .000060388 to .000145799. All twelve intersections pass an independent edge/triangle interior test, and the longest crossing segment is .022372340. The two old r02a pair keys do not persist. These are new local overlaps, not merely inherited contacts or roundoff.

The implicated stable IDs are 2466–2469, 2960–2963, 3671–3674, 4392–4394, 122088, 122089 and 122123. Full triangle pairs, intersection centers, segment lengths and pre-fit surface gaps are in r02d-intersection-details.json. Stable IDs refer to the before/after NPZ row identity, verified against the GLBs by exact bijections.

Keeping the PCA-smoothed result and repairing this small strip is better supported than backing off the entire fit. If the strip is deformed again without rebuilding it, use a coherent local direction field and constrain neighboring relative movement against triangle width and nearby-surface clearance. A globally small displacement bound alone is inadequate. If angular smoothing is used, preserve the low-frequency center, mean radius and ovality, and apply it to a consistent circumferential parameterization. Independently rounding Y sections would misinterpret the bent root's obliquity. The original irregular root mesh is better served by the demonstrated PCA approach than by forcing every section onto a circle.

One further mechanism remains a hypothesis. The fit's hard radius .05 truncates a Gaussian with sigma .03 while the kernel still has about .249 of its central scalar weight. Near-coincident vertices can therefore admit different finite-weight neighbors. For edge 2467–2468, original length .000069916, the radius-.05 neighborhoods differ by one vertex and the final relative displacement is .000063370. This discontinuous support may contribute to the remaining tiny-strip overlap. It is not isolated causally by these measurements. A compact kernel that reaches zero at the support boundary would address that possibility, but local cleanup is the direct response to the measured defect.

The audit scripts are read-only CPU programs. diagnose_ribs.py reproduces the native-pass and profile measurements. diagnose_mls_folds.py compares r02c face orientations and local intersections. validate_surface_candidates.py validates r02d against r02a and r02c, and exits nonzero while its local intersection check fails. inspect_intersection_pairs.py independently confirms the twelve crossings.
